# Crystal Rush

Collect crystals by touching them, return to the green deposit pad to convert
carried crystals into coins, and use the blue upgrade pad to buy walk speed.

## V1 acceptance

1. A player can carry at most five crystals; collected crystals respawn.
2. Depositing grants ten coins per carried crystal and clears the carry count.
3. The speed upgrade costs 30 coins and adds four WalkSpeed, up to three times.
4. The HUD displays crystals, coins, speed level, and round time.
5. The server exclusively mutates gameplay state.
